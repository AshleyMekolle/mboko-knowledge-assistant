## Purpose

This procedure explains how Mboko Media Group hires new employees, welcomes them and removes their access when they leave.

These are fictional company rules created for a portfolio project. They are not legal advice.

## Ownership

The People and Administration department manages recruitment, onboarding and offboarding. The People and Administration Manager (Ruth Fon) owns this procedure. Finance and Operations is responsible for laptops, system accounts, final payments and asset returns. The hiring Department Head is responsible for the new employee's role, training plan and probation review.

## Recruitment

1. **Request.** The Department Head submits a hiring request in MbokoHR with the job title, responsibilities, reasons for the role and proposed start date.
2. **Approval.** The Managing Director approves the new position. The Finance and Operations Manager confirms the cost is in the approved budget.
3. **Advertise.** The People and Administration Manager publishes the job description on LinkedIn and Mboko's careers page for at least 10 working days.
4. **Shortlist.** The Department Head and the People and Administration Manager shortlist candidates within 5 working days after the closing date.
5. **Interview.** Shortlisted candidates have at least one interview with the Department Head and the People and Administration Manager. Creative and editorial roles include a practical task.
6. **References.** Two references are checked before an offer is made.
7. **Offer.** The Managing Director approves the offer. The People and Administration Manager sends the written offer and contract.
8. **Records.** Unsuccessful candidates are informed in writing. Applications are deleted 6 months after the role is filled.

## Before the first day

Once the contract is signed, the People and Administration Manager:
1. Creates the employee record in MbokoHR.
2. Submits an access request to Finance and Operations **at least 5 working days before the start date**, listing the systems the role needs.
3. Asks the Department Head to prepare the first-week plan and name an onboarding buddy.

Finance and Operations (Aïcha Bello) then prepares the laptop, records it in the asset inventory and creates accounts for company email, Mboko Drive, Mboko Board and MbokoHR. Access to Mboko Ledger, MBK-NAS-01 and social media accounts is given only to roles that need it, approved by the Finance and Operations Manager.

## Onboarding

### First day checklist

| Task | Responsible |
|---|---|
| Welcome and office tour, safety and fire exits | People and Administration Manager |
| Sign the confidentiality agreement and receive the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001) | People and Administration Manager |
| Collect laptop, sign the asset receipt, log in to all accounts | Finance and Operations Officer |
| Meet the team and the onboarding buddy | Department Head |
| Review the first-week plan | Department Head |

### First week: induction training

Every new employee completes these sessions in the first 5 working days:
1. Company overview, values and departments (COM-REF-001), with the People and Administration Manager.
2. Editorial standards and consent (EDIT-POL-001 and EDIT-POL-002), with the Editor-in-Chief.
3. Production safety and incident reporting (PROD-POL-001), with the Head of Production.
4. Expenses, purchasing and approvals (FIN-POL-001, FIN-SOP-001 and COM-POL-001), with the Finance and Operations Manager.
5. Brand guidelines (CREA-STD-001), with the Creative Director.

The People and Administration Manager records completed sessions in MbokoHR.

### Probation

- New employees have a **3-month probation period**.
- The Department Head holds check-in meetings at 30, 60 and 90 days and records them in MbokoHR.
- At 90 days, the Department Head recommends confirming the employment, extending probation once by up to 3 months, or ending the employment. The People and Administration Manager confirms the outcome in writing.

## Offboarding

### When an employee leaves

1. The employee gives written notice to their Department Head and the People and Administration Manager. The notice period is stated in the employment contract.
2. The People and Administration Manager confirms the last working day in writing and informs Finance and Operations the same day.
3. The Department Head agrees a handover plan for current projects, including updating Mboko Board and moving files out of any personal folders into the project folders.

### Return of equipment

- All company equipment, including laptop, production equipment, keys and access badge, is returned to the Finance and Operations Officer on or before the last working day.
- Production equipment is returned through the Production Coordinator under the Equipment Checkout and Return Policy (PROD-POL-002).
- The Finance and Operations Officer signs the clearance form once every item is returned.

### Access revocation

- The Finance and Operations Manager removes access to **all systems and accounts by 17:30 on the last working day**, including email, Mboko Drive, Mboko Board, MbokoHR, Mboko Ledger, MBK-NAS-01 and any social media accounts.
- Passwords for shared accounts the employee could access, such as social media accounts, are changed the same day.
- If an employee is dismissed, access is removed immediately when the employee is informed.

### Final payment and records

- Final pay, including payment for unused annual leave from the current year, is made after the clearance form is signed.
- The People and Administration Manager holds an exit conversation and keeps the employee record for 5 years, as stated in the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001).
