## Purpose

This handbook gives every Mboko Media Group employee an overview of how the company works and where to find the detailed rules. Where a topic has its own policy, the handbook summarises it and the detailed policy applies.

These are fictional company rules created for a portfolio project. They are not legal advice and have not been checked against Cameroonian employment law.

## Who this handbook applies to

All employees of Mboko Media Group. Freelancers and vendors working on Mboko productions must follow the sections on conduct, confidentiality and safety.

## Working hours

- Standard working hours are 08:30 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a lunch break from 12:30 to 13:30.
- Production employees work shoot-day hours set on the call sheet. Time worked on a shoot day beyond 10 hours is recovered as time off, agreed with the Head of Production within 30 days.
- Attendance and absence rules are in the Leave and Attendance Policy (HR-POL-002).

## Workplace conduct

All employees are expected to:
1. Treat colleagues, contributors, clients and members of the public with respect.
2. Never harass, bully, discriminate against or intimidate anyone, at the office, on location or online.
3. Declare any conflict of interest, such as a family link to a contributor, client or vendor, to their Department Head before working on the related project.
4. Never accept gifts, cash or favours worth more than XAF 25,000 from a contributor, client or vendor. Smaller gifts must be declared to the Department Head.
5. Never pay a contributor for an interview in editorial content without the Editor-in-Chief's approval.
6. Use company equipment, systems and money only for Mboko work.
7. Not drink alcohol or use drugs at work or on a shoot.

### Personal social media

Employees may say they work at Mboko on personal accounts. They must not share unpublished content, confidential information or contributors' private details, and must not present personal opinions as Mboko's position.

## Complaints and escalation

1. **Informal.** Raise the concern with your Department Head, if you feel comfortable doing so.
2. **Formal.** Submit a written complaint to the People and Administration Manager (Ruth Fon). Ruth acknowledges it within 2 working days and agrees next steps with the employee.
3. **If the complaint involves your Department Head or the People and Administration Manager,** submit it directly to the Managing Director.
4. **Investigation.** Formal complaints are investigated within 15 working days of being acknowledged. The outcome is given to the employee in writing.
5. **Confidentiality.** Complaints are handled confidentially. No employee may be treated unfairly for raising a complaint in good faith.
6. **Safety.** Any immediate threat to safety is reported to the Managing Director at once.

## Confidentiality

- Unpublished content, contributor information, client contracts, budgets and employee records are confidential.
- Confidential information must be stored only in Mboko systems and shared only with people who need it for their work.
- Confidentiality continues after an employee leaves Mboko.

## Employee records

- Employee records are kept in MbokoHR and managed by the People and Administration Manager.
- Records include the employment contract, job title, start date, emergency contact, leave records, training records and performance reviews.
- Access is limited to the employee, their Department Head (for leave, training and performance only), the People and Administration Manager and the Managing Director. The Finance and Operations Manager may access payroll information only.
- Employees may ask to see their own record and request corrections through MbokoHR.
- Records are kept for 5 years after an employee leaves.

| Team chat | Day-to-day questions and quick updates |
| Mboko Board | Project status, approvals, requests and handovers |

| Monthly all-hands, first Friday of the month | Company updates from the Managing Director |

Approvals given in team chat are not valid. They must be recorded in the system named in the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001).

## Training and development

- Each employee has a training plan agreed with their Department Head during the annual review in January.
- Each employee may attend up to 5 working days of approved training per year.
- Training costs are approved under the financial limits in COM-POL-001.
- All new employees complete induction training on editorial standards, consent, safety and finance procedures during their first week, as described in HR-SOP-001.

## Document control for the knowledge base

- Each document in this knowledge base has one owner, named in its metadata table.
- Only the document owner may change a document. Each change increases the version number, updates the "Last reviewed date" and adds a row to the revision history.
- Owners review their documents at least once a year.
- The People and Administration Manager keeps the knowledge base index (COM-IDX-001) up to date.
- If two documents conflict, the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001) applies for approvals, and the owning department's document applies for everything else. Report conflicts to the People and Administration Manager.
