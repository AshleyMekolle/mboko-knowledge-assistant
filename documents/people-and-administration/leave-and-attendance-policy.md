## Purpose

This policy explains how much leave Mboko Media Group employees receive, how to request it and what to do when absent.

These are fictional company rules created for a portfolio project. They are not legal advice and have not been checked against Cameroonian employment law.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees. It does not apply to freelancers.

## Annual leave entitlement

- Every full-time employee receives **24 working days of paid annual leave** per calendar year.
- Leave builds up at 2 working days for each full month worked.
- New employees may take leave once they have completed their first month.
- Employees who join part-way through the year receive leave for the months remaining.

## How to request annual leave

1. Submit the request in MbokoHR with the dates and the number of working days.
2. Notice required:
   - **1 to 3 working days of leave:** at least **3 working days** before the first day of leave.
   - **More than 3 working days of leave:** at least **10 working days** before the first day of leave.
3. The employee's Department Head approves or declines the request in MbokoHR within 2 working days. Department Heads' leave is approved by the Managing Director.
4. Leave is confirmed only when it shows as "Approved" in MbokoHR. Verbal agreement is not enough.
5. Before leave starts, the employee hands over urgent work to a named colleague and sets an out-of-office message.

### Leave on shoot days and busy periods

- Production employees may not take leave on scheduled shoot days in the project register (PROD-POL-001) unless the Head of Production approves it.
- Editorial and Partnerships and Marketing employees should avoid leave in the week before a season launch or major campaign launch. Department Heads may decline leave in these weeks.

## Carrying over leave

- Up to 5 unused days of annual leave may be carried into the next year.
- Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the next year, or they are lost.
- Unused leave is not paid out, except when an employee leaves Mboko, as described in HR-SOP-001.

## Public holidays

Mboko observes the official public holidays of Cameroon. Employees who work on a public holiday for a scheduled shoot receive a replacement day off, taken within 30 days and agreed with the Head of Production.

## Sick leave

1. Inform your Department Head by phone or message **before 09:00** on the first day of absence.
2. Record the absence in MbokoHR when you return.
3. A medical certificate is required for absences of **more than 2 consecutive working days**. Upload it to MbokoHR within 2 working days of returning.
4. If you are sick on a shoot day, also inform the Production Coordinator immediately so the shoot can be re-planned.

## Compassionate leave

Employees receive up to **3 working days** of paid compassionate leave on the death of a close family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or grandparent). Request it in MbokoHR. Department Heads may approve it at short notice.

## Other leave

Parental leave and other types of statutory leave are granted according to applicable law. Contact the People and Administration Manager for arrangements.

## Attendance and lateness

- Employees who will arrive after 09:00 must inform their Department Head before 09:00.
- Absence without notice for 2 consecutive working days is referred to the People and Administration Manager.
- Repeated lateness or unauthorised absence is handled first by the Department Head and then, if it continues, under the complaints and escalation process in the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001).

## Records

Leave requests, approvals, sick leave and medical certificates are kept in MbokoHR by the People and Administration Manager as part of the employee record.
