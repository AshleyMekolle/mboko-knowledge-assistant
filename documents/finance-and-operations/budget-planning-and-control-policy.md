## Purpose

This policy explains how Mboko Media Group plans its budgets, approves spending against them, controls changes and closes its accounts each month. It also contains the budget and expense register, which is the authoritative record of project budgets and spending. All figures are fictional and in XAF.

## Scope

This policy applies to the annual company budget, every project budget and every department budget.

## Annual budget

1. The Finance and Operations Manager sends the budget template to Department Heads by **1 November** each year.
2. Department Heads submit their requests by **20 November**.
3. The Finance and Operations Manager prepares the draft company budget and reviews it with the Managing Director.
4. The Managing Director approves the annual budget by **15 December**.
5. The approved budget is loaded into Mboko Ledger before 1 January.

## Project budgets

1. Every new production or branded project needs a project budget before it is greenlit. The producer prepares it, using budget lines for crew, equipment, travel, post-production, translation, music and licensing, and contingency.
2. Every project budget includes a **contingency line of 5%**.
3. The Finance and Operations Manager reviews the budget, and the Managing Director approves it, as set out in the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001).
4. Branded projects must show the client fee and the cost, and the cost must not exceed 75% of the client fee unless the Managing Director approves it.
5. Grants and sponsorship income linked to a project are recorded against that project.

## Spending against a budget

- Every purchase, expense claim and petty cash payment must be linked to a project code or department budget line.
- Spending is approved at the limits in COM-POL-001, using the Expense Claims and Petty Cash Policy (FIN-POL-001) and the Procurement, Vendor and Invoice Procedure (FIN-SOP-001).
- Costs above XAF 2,000,000 can only be approved if they are already included in an approved budget.

## Budget changes and variances

| Change | Approver |
|---|---|
| Moving money between budget lines in one project, up to XAF 500,000 | Finance and Operations Manager |
| Moving more than XAF 500,000 between budget lines | Managing Director |
| Increasing a project's total budget by more than 10% or more than XAF 500,000, whichever is lower | Managing Director |
| Using contingency | Producer, informing the Finance and Operations Manager |

When a project is forecast to go over budget, the producer informs the Finance and Operations Manager within 2 working days and proposes savings or a budget change.

## Monthly financial close

The monthly close is completed by the **5th working day of the following month**.

1. **Working day 1:** All departments submit outstanding expense claims and confirm GRNs for goods and services received during the month.
2. **Working day 2:** The Finance and Operations Officer records all invoices, claims and petty cash, and reconciles the bank accounts and petty cash.
3. **Working day 3:** The Finance and Operations Officer lists invoices on hold and accruals for costs received but not yet invoiced.
4. **Working day 4:** The Finance and Operations Manager reviews the trial balance and project spending against budget.
5. **Working day 5:** The Finance and Operations Manager sends the monthly finance report to the Managing Director and each producer, and updates the register below.
