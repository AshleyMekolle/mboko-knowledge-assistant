> **Fictional document.** Mboko Media Group is a fictional company created for a software engineering portfolio project. All people, clients, vendors, figures and records in this document are invented. Nothing here is legal, financial or employment advice.

## Purpose

This policy explains how employees are reimbursed for money they spend on Mboko Media Group work, and how petty cash is used and controlled. All amounts are fictional and in XAF.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees. Freelancers claim expenses only if their contract allows it, using the same rules.

## Principles

- Spend company money as carefully as your own.
- Only claim costs that were necessary for Mboko work and linked to a project code or department budget.
- Use a purchase order instead of paying personally for anything above XAF 100,000, as required by the Procurement, Vendor and Invoice Procedure (FIN-SOP-001).
- Nobody may approve their own claim.

## Submitting an expense claim

1. Submit the claim in Mboko Ledger **within 30 calendar days of the date the expense was paid**. Claims submitted later are rejected unless the Finance and Operations Manager approves an exception.
2. Enter one line per expense with the date, amount, description, project code and budget line.
3. Attach the supporting documents listed below.
4. The claim goes to the approvers set out in the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001):

| Total claim (XAF) | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to 100,000 | Department Head |
| 100,001 to 500,000 | Department Head and Finance and Operations Manager |
| 500,001 to 2,000,000 | Department Head, Finance and Operations Manager and Managing Director |

A Department Head's claim is approved by the Managing Director in place of the Department Head.

5. The Finance and Operations Officer (Aïcha Bello) checks the claim against this policy within 3 working days of final approval.
6. Approved claims are paid by bank transfer **within 10 working days of final approval**, in the next Thursday payment run.

## Supporting documents required for reimbursement

| Expense | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Any purchase above XAF 3,000 | Original receipt or invoice showing the vendor name, date, items and amount |
| Local transport up to XAF 3,000 per trip without a receipt, such as moto-taxi | Entry in the signed transport log with date, route, purpose and amount |
| Accommodation | Hotel invoice in the employee's name or Mboko's name |
| Meals with contributors or clients | Receipt plus the names of the people present and the purpose |
| Fuel for vehicles | Fuel receipt plus the vehicle registration and trip purpose |
| Lost receipt | Signed lost-receipt declaration, approved by the Finance and Operations Manager. Allowed at most twice per year per employee |

Claims without the required evidence are returned to the employee and are not paid.

## Travel and per diem rates

These rates apply to work travel outside Douala within Cameroon.

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Meals per diem | XAF 15,000 per day away from Douala, without receipts |
| Accommodation | Up to XAF 35,000 per night, with an invoice |
| Intercity travel | Bus or shared vehicle fares with a ticket, or vehicle hire through Littoral Fleet Services (vendor V-004) on a purchase order |
| Local transport in Douala | Actual cost, with receipts or the transport log |

Per diem is not paid for days when meals are provided by Mboko, a client or a partner. Travel outside Douala for a shoot must be in the approved Production Plan (PROD-POL-001).

## What cannot be claimed

- Alcohol, except at a client meal approved in advance by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing.
- Fines, including traffic fines.
- Personal items, personal travel and upgrades.
- Payments to contributors for interviews, unless approved by the Editor-in-Chief.

## Petty cash

Petty cash is for small, urgent purchases that cannot wait for a claim or purchase order, such as batteries before a shoot.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Custodian | Aïcha Bello, Finance and Operations Officer |
| Petty cash float | XAF 150,000, kept in the locked cash box at the Douala office |
| Maximum single payment | XAF 25,000 |
| Replenishment | When the balance falls below XAF 40,000, approved by the Finance and Operations Manager |
| Cash count | Every Friday by the custodian, checked by the Finance and Operations Manager once a month |

### How petty cash is used

1. The employee asks the custodian for cash and signs a petty cash voucher with the amount, purpose and project code.
2. Advances must be settled with a receipt and any change **within 2 working days**.
3. The custodian records every payment and settlement in the petty cash book in Mboko Ledger.
4. Purchases above XAF 25,000 must use an expense claim or a purchase order, not petty cash.
5. Any difference found in a cash count is reported to the Finance and Operations Manager on the same day.

## Exceptions and escalation

Exceptions to this policy need written approval from the Finance and Operations Manager. Suspected misuse of company money is reported to the Managing Director.
