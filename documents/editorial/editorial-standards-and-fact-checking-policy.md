> **Fictional document.** Mboko Media Group is a fictional company created for a software engineering portfolio project. All people, clients, vendors, figures and records in this document are invented. Nothing here is legal, financial or employment advice.

## Purpose

This policy sets the editorial standards every Mboko Media Group story must meet and defines how research and fact-checking are carried out before anything is published.

## Scope

This policy applies to all documentaries, series episodes, interviews, articles, social posts with factual claims and branded content. Branded and sponsored content must meet the same accuracy standards as editorial content.

## Ownership

The Editorial department owns this policy and is responsible for fact-checking every documentary, series episode and article. Production supplies footage logs and transcripts, and Partnerships and Marketing supplies client briefs, but responsibility for accuracy stays with Editorial.

## Editorial standards

1. **Accuracy.** Every factual claim must be verified before publication. When a fact cannot be verified, it must be removed or clearly attributed, for example "according to the market traders' association".
2. **Fairness.** People and organisations facing criticism or allegations must be given a fair opportunity to respond before publication. The request and any reply are recorded in the Fact-Check Report.
3. **Respect and dignity.** Contributors are portrayed as they are, without mockery, exaggeration or staged events presented as real.
4. **Consent.** No contributor is published without a valid consent record under the Interview Consent and Contributor Release Policy (EDIT-POL-002).
5. **Independence.** Sponsors and clients cannot approve, change or remove editorial content in Mboko's editorial productions. Sponsorship arrangements are described in the Sponsorship and Campaign Workflow (MKT-SOP-001).
6. **Clear labelling.** Branded content and sponsored segments are clearly labelled as such to audiences.
7. **Harm reduction.** Mboko does not publish information that could put a contributor at risk, such as the home location of a vulnerable person, without the Editor-in-Chief's explicit approval.
8. **No fabrication.** Quotes are never invented or altered in meaning. Translated quotes must keep the original meaning.

## High-risk content

High-risk content is content involving allegations against named people or organisations, minors, health or legal claims, or contributors who could be put at risk by publication. High-risk content requires approval from both the Editor-in-Chief and the Managing Director before publication. The Editor-in-Chief may also request review by Mboko's external legal adviser.

## Research

1. Research starts when a story is set to "Research" in Mboko Board.
2. The Researcher and Fact-Checker (Linda Achu) builds a research file in Mboko Drive under `/Editorial/{ProjectCode}/Research/` with sources, contacts and background.
3. Contributors are checked before filming: identity, role or business, and any reason they may be at risk if featured.
4. The Editor-in-Chief confirms that research is complete before the Head of Production schedules a shoot, as required by PROD-POL-001.

## Fact-checking procedure

### What must be checked

Every factual claim: names and titles, places, dates, numbers and prices, quotations, historical and cultural statements, statements about businesses and organisations, and claims made by contributors that are presented as fact.

### Sources

| Claim type | Minimum verification |
|---|---|
| Names, titles and spellings | Confirmed with the person or an official document |
| Numbers, statistics and prices | One authoritative source, such as a government agency, official report or the organisation itself |
| Contested, sensitive or surprising claims | Two independent sources, or one primary document |
| Historical and cultural claims | One published expert source, or a recorded interview with a recognised custodian of that knowledge, such as a traditional leader for Racines |
| Contributor opinions | No verification needed, but must be presented as opinion |

### Steps

1. The writer or producer submits the script or article in Mboko Board when its status is set to "Editorial Review".
2. The Researcher and Fact-Checker lists every factual claim in a Fact-Check Report and records how each one was verified, with links to sources.
3. Claims that fail verification are marked "Remove", "Rewrite" or "Attribute" and returned to the writer or producer.
4. When every claim is resolved, the Researcher and Fact-Checker marks the Fact-Check Report "Complete" in Mboko Board.
5. Content cannot receive Final Editorial Sign-off until the Fact-Check Report is "Complete".

### Turnaround times

| Content | Fact-check turnaround |
|---|---|
| Article or social post | 1 working day |
| Episode script up to 15 minutes | 2 working days |
| Episode script longer than 15 minutes | 4 working days |
| Branded film script | 2 working days |

### Example

For Mboa Makers episode MBK-MM-S02E03, a contributor said her soap business "employs 40 women in Bonabéri". The Researcher and Fact-Checker confirmed the number with the business's staff list, and recorded the source in the Fact-Check Report. A second claim, that Bonabéri was "the largest artisan district in Cameroon", could not be verified and was rewritten as the contributor's opinion.
