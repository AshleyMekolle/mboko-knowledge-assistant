> **Fictional document.** Mboko Media Group is a fictional company created for a software engineering portfolio project. All people, clients, vendors, figures and records in this document are invented. Nothing here is legal, financial or employment advice.

## Purpose

This policy makes sure that everyone who appears in or contributes to Mboko Media Group content has given informed consent, and that the consent is recorded and can be found later.

## Scope

This policy applies to every interview, filmed contributor, recorded voice, photograph of an identifiable person and contributor quote used in Mboko content, including branded content and social media.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Editor-in-Chief | Owns this policy and approves consent for minors and other exceptions. |
| Researcher and Fact-Checker | Explains the project to contributors during research and prepares consent forms. |
| Producer and Production Coordinator | Collect signed consent on the shoot day and include consent records in the Handover Package (PROD-SOP-001). |
| Bilingual Editor | Makes sure contributors receive the consent form in the language they understand, English or French. |

## Informed consent

Consent is informed only when the contributor understands, before recording:
- who Mboko Media Group is and which production they are taking part in;
- how their contribution will be used, including which platforms and languages;
- that the content may be edited, translated and subtitled;
- whether the content is editorial or branded, and the name of the client for branded content;
- their right to withdraw, described below.

The explanation must be given in English or French, whichever the contributor prefers. If the contributor speaks another language, an interpreter must explain the form, and the interpreter's name is recorded.

## What a consent record must contain

Every consent record must include:
1. Contributor's full name and the name they want used on screen, if different.
2. Date and location of the recording.
3. Project code and episode, for example MBK-MV-S03E06.
4. Description of how the content will be used: platforms, languages and whether it is editorial or branded.
5. Whether the contributor agrees to be identified by face, by name, both, or neither.
6. The withdrawal deadline.
7. The contributor's signature, or a recorded verbal consent as described below.
8. Name and signature of the Mboko employee who took the consent.
9. For minors, the signature of a parent or legal guardian.

## Written and recorded verbal consent

- Written consent on the Mboko consent form is the default.
- Recorded verbal consent may be used when the contributor cannot read or sign the form. The employee reads the full form aloud on camera, and the contributor states their name, the date and their agreement on camera. The clip's file name is recorded in the consent record.

## Minors

A minor is anyone under 18 years old. Featuring a minor requires:
- written consent from a parent or legal guardian;
- the minor's own agreement, given in words they understand;
- approval from the Editor-in-Chief before filming;
- treatment as high-risk content under the Editorial Standards and Fact-Checking Policy (EDIT-POL-001), which means the Managing Director also approves before publication.

## Withdrawing consent

- A contributor may withdraw consent at any time **up to 7 calendar days before the scheduled publication date**. Mboko will then remove their contribution.
- After that deadline, the Editor-in-Chief will still consider requests to remove or blur a contributor, especially if the contributor reports a risk to their safety. Safety risks are always treated as urgent.
- Withdrawal requests are recorded in Mboko Board with the date and the action taken.

## Branded content

Contributors in branded content, such as the Kribi Coffee Collective film (MBK-BR-KCC-01), must be told the name of the client and that the content is advertising. Client employees who appear also need a consent record.

## Storage and retention

- Consent records are stored in Mboko Drive under `/Editorial/Consent/{ProjectCode}/{EpisodeCode}/`.
- They are kept for as long as the content is published, plus 5 years.
- Only Editorial, the producer of the project and the Managing Director may access consent records.

## Consequences of missing consent

Content without a valid consent record for every identifiable contributor cannot move to Editorial Review, as stated in the Footage Storage and Handover Procedure (PROD-SOP-001). If consent cannot be obtained, the contributor must be removed or made unidentifiable.
