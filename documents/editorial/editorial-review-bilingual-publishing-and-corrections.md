## Purpose

This procedure defines the steps content must pass before publication, how English and French versions are produced and approved, and how errors in published content are corrected or retracted.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all video, articles and social posts published by Mboko Media Group, including branded and sponsored content.

## Editorial review and approval steps

Content moves through these steps in Mboko Board. A step cannot start until the previous one is complete.

| Step | What happens | Who | Time allowed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Handover received | Editorial confirms the Handover Package is complete, including consent records | Editor-in-Chief or delegate | 1 working day |
| 2. Rough cut review | Producer reviews structure and story | Producer | 2 working days |
| 3. Fact-check | Fact-Check Report completed under EDIT-POL-001 | Researcher and Fact-Checker | See EDIT-POL-001 |
| 4. Fine cut review | Editorial review of accuracy, fairness, tone and consent | Editor-in-Chief | 2 working days |
| 5. Translation and subtitles | Second-language version produced and reviewed | Bilingual Editor | See below |
| 6. Creative assets | Thumbnails, titles and graphics approved | Creative Director | Under CREA-STD-001 |
| 7. Final Editorial Sign-off | Editor-in-Chief approves the final version in each language | Editor-in-Chief | 1 working day |
| 8. High-risk approval | Only for high-risk content | Managing Director | 2 working days |
| 9. Approved for Publication | Status set in Mboko Board when steps 1 to 8 are complete | Editor-in-Chief | Same day |

For branded and sponsored campaigns, the client's written approval and the final publication approval by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing are also required, as described in the Sponsorship and Campaign Workflow (MKT-SOP-001).

**Who approves a video before publication:** the Editor-in-Chief gives Final Editorial Sign-off on every video. High-risk videos also need the Managing Director. Branded and sponsored videos also need the client's written approval and the Head of Partnerships and Marketing.

### When French content or translation is required

- Every video must be published with subtitles in the second language listed above.
- Every article is published in its original language. A translation is required when the article accompanies a video that is published in both languages.
- Social posts for each video are written in both English and French.
- Contributor interviews in local languages, such as Duala or Bamoun, are subtitled in both English and French.

### Approval steps for content published in both English and French

1. The Bilingual Editor (Claire Mvondo) produces the translation or subtitles from the fine cut approved at step 4.
2. A second fluent reader, either the Editor-in-Chief or the translation vendor Sawa Translations (vendor V-003), checks the translation against the original.
3. The Bilingual Editor approves the translation in Mboko Board.
4. The Editor-in-Chief gives Final Editorial Sign-off on **each language version separately**.
5. Both versions are published at the same time. Publishing one language first requires the Editor-in-Chief's approval.

## Corrections and retractions

### Types of error

| Type | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Spelling, grammar or formatting error that does not change meaning | A typo in a caption |
| Significant | A factual error that changes meaning but does not cause serious harm | A wrong date, name, number or place |
| Serious | An error that could cause serious harm, damage a person's reputation or expose Mboko to legal risk | A wrong allegation about a named business |

### How to handle a factual error in published content

1. **Report.** Anyone who finds or receives a report of a possible error informs the Editor-in-Chief and the Researcher and Fact-Checker on the same working day.
2. **Verify.** The Researcher and Fact-Checker checks the claim and reports the finding to the Editor-in-Chief within 1 working day.
3. **Decide.** The Editor-in-Chief classifies the error and approves the correction.
4. **Correct.**
   - Minor errors are fixed without a correction note.
   - Significant errors are corrected **within 24 hours of the error being confirmed**. A correction note is added at the end of the article or video description: "Correction, [date]: An earlier version of this [video or article] stated [error]. The correct information is [fact]."
   - Serious errors: the content is unpublished immediately while the Editor-in-Chief and the Managing Director decide whether to correct or retract it.
5. **Retract.** A retraction removes the content and publishes a retraction notice explaining why. Retractions require the Editor-in-Chief and the Managing Director.
6. **Social posts.** The Social Media and Audience Manager corrects or removes related social posts and pins the correction note where the platform allows.
7. **Both languages.** Corrections are applied to both the English and French versions.
8. **Record.** The Researcher and Fact-Checker records every correction or retraction in the Corrections Log.
