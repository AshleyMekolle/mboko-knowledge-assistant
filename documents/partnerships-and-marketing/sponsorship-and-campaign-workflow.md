## Purpose

This procedure explains how Mboko Media Group manages clients, sponsors and partners from first contact to final campaign report. It defines the approvals every campaign needs before publication and how client feedback and change requests are handled.

## Scope

This procedure covers sponsorships of Mboko productions, branded content made for clients, and media partnerships.

## Client onboarding

1. The Head of Partnerships and Marketing records the client in the campaign register below with a client ID.
2. The client provides: legal name, NIU taxpayer number, billing contact, approval contact and its brand guidelines.
3. The Head of Partnerships and Marketing confirms that the client's activity does not conflict with Mboko's values or editorial independence. Mboko does not accept sponsorship from tobacco, gambling or political parties.
4. The Finance and Operations Manager sets the client up in Mboko Ledger for invoicing.

## Proposals and contracts

1. The Head of Partnerships and Marketing prepares the proposal: objectives, deliverables, timeline, price and the number of revision rounds.
2. The Producer and the Creative Director confirm the deliverables are achievable before the proposal is sent.
3. Approval: proposals up to XAF 10,000,000 are approved by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing. Proposals above XAF 10,000,000 also need the Managing Director, as set out in the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001).
4. Every contract is signed by the Managing Director. No production work starts before the contract is signed.
5. Every sponsorship contract includes an **editorial independence clause**: the sponsor receives credits and agreed promotional placements but cannot review, approve or change editorial content.

## Campaign stages

Each campaign has one status in Mboko Board: Planning, Contracted, In Production, In Internal Review, In Client Review, Approved for Publication, Live, Reporting, Closed.

## Approvals before a campaign is published

A sponsored or branded campaign may only be published when **every** approval below is recorded in Mboko Board, in this order:

1. Fact-Check Report complete and Final Editorial Sign-off by the Editor-in-Chief, under EDIT-SOP-001.
2. Creative assets approved by the Creative Director, and client-facing assets also approved by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing, under CREA-STD-001.
3. **Client's written approval** of the final versions. For branded content, this is the client's approval of the content. For sponsorships, it covers only the sponsor credits and sponsored posts, never the editorial content.
4. **Final campaign publication approval** by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing.

Only then does the status change to "Approved for Publication" and the Social Media and Audience Manager may schedule it under the Social Media Publishing Policy (MKT-POL-001). No campaign may be scheduled or published before all four approvals are complete.

## Responsibilities of Partnerships and Marketing during a sponsored campaign

1. Keep the sponsor informed with a status update every two weeks.
2. Make sure the sponsor credit and logo appear exactly as the contract states, with written logo permission on file.
3. Protect editorial independence by routing any sponsor comment on editorial content to the Editor-in-Chief, who decides whether to respond.
4. Coordinate sponsored social posts with the Social Media and Audience Manager.
5. Inform the sponsor within 2 working days if a deliverable date changes, under the at-risk process in PROD-POL-001.
6. Deliver the performance reports described below.
7. Confirm with Finance and Operations that each invoice milestone has been invoiced.

## Client feedback and change requests

1. Clients give feedback in writing, by email or in the review link, to the Head of Partnerships and Marketing only.
2. Each contract includes **two rounds of revisions**. Feedback for one round must be consolidated into one document.
3. Mboko responds to each round of feedback within 5 working days, unless the producer agrees a different date.
4. **A change request** is any request outside the agreed brief or beyond the included revision rounds, such as a new script, a new shoot day or extra deliverables.
5. For a change request, the Head of Partnerships and Marketing confirms the cost and schedule impact with the producer, sends a written change order to the client, and starts work only after the client accepts it in writing. Added costs are approved under the financial limits in COM-POL-001.
6. Feedback that would make content inaccurate or misleading is declined, and the Editor-in-Chief explains the reason to the client.

## Campaign performance reporting

| Report | When | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Interim report | Every 4 weeks while the campaign is Live | Views, reach, engagement rate, top content, audience comments summary |
| Final report | Within 15 working days after the campaign ends | All interim metrics, results against the agreed objectives, lessons and recommendations |

Reports are prepared by the Social Media and Audience Manager, approved by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing and stored in Mboko Drive under `/Marketing/{CampaignID}/Reports/`. Metrics are taken from each platform's own analytics. Engagement rate is calculated as (likes + comments + shares + saves) divided by reach.
