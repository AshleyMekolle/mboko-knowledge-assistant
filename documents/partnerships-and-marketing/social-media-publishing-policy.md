## Purpose

This policy controls what Mboko Media Group publishes on its official social and video channels, who may publish it, and how comments, mistakes and crises are handled.

## Scope

This policy applies to all official Mboko accounts and to anyone who publishes on them. Employees' personal accounts are covered by the conduct rules in the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001).

## Official channels

| Channel | Main use | Language |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Full episodes and branded films | Original language with subtitles in the second language |
| Instagram | Short clips, behind the scenes, sponsored posts | English and French |
| Facebook | Episodes, clips and community discussion | English and French |
| TikTok | Short clips, especially Rues de Douala | French, with English captions |
| LinkedIn | Company news, partnerships and recruitment | English |

## Who may publish

- Only the Social Media and Audience Manager (Patrick Njoya) and the Head of Partnerships and Marketing (Joëlle Essomba) have publishing access to official accounts.
- Account access is managed by the Finance and Operations Manager, who keeps the account credentials in the company password manager. Passwords must never be shared by message or email.
- Access is removed on an employee's last working day, as described in the Recruitment, Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure (HR-SOP-001).

## Pre-publication checklist

Before scheduling any post, the Social Media and Audience Manager confirms that:
1. The content's status in Mboko Board is **"Approved for Publication"**.
2. The Editor-in-Chief has given Final Editorial Sign-off for each language version.
3. The Creative Director has approved the thumbnail and graphics, and the file name ends in `_FINAL`.
4. For branded or sponsored campaigns, the client's written approval and the final campaign publication approval by the Head of Partnerships and Marketing are recorded, as required by the Sponsorship and Campaign Workflow (MKT-SOP-001).
5. Branded and sponsored posts are labelled using the platform's paid partnership label and the words "Sponsored" or "Partenariat".
6. The post copy is available in both English and French where required by EDIT-SOP-001.

If any item is missing, the post must not be scheduled.

## Who approves a social media campaign before publication

For Mboko's own editorial content, the Editor-in-Chief's Final Editorial Sign-off allows publication. For a branded or sponsored campaign, the final approval before publication is given by the **Head of Partnerships and Marketing**, after Final Editorial Sign-off, Creative Director approval and the client's written approval.

## Scheduling

- Posts are scheduled in each platform's scheduling tool at least 24 hours before publication.
- Episodes publish on the dates in the project register (PROD-POL-001). Mboa Makers publishes every second Monday, Market Voices every Wednesday and Rues de Douala every Friday.
- Bilingual posts publish at the same time in both languages unless the Editor-in-Chief approves otherwise.

## Comment moderation

- Comments are checked at least twice per working day.
- Hide or remove comments that contain hate speech, threats, harassment, spam, private information about contributors, or scams.
- Never remove a comment only because it criticises Mboko.
- Comments pointing out a possible error are sent to the Editor-in-Chief on the same day, under the corrections process in EDIT-SOP-001.
- Contributors who are targeted by abuse are contacted by the producer to offer support, and the Editor-in-Chief decides whether to turn off comments.

## Mistakes and crises

1. A post published with an error is corrected or removed following the corrections process in EDIT-SOP-001.
2. A post published before all approvals were recorded is removed immediately, and the Head of Partnerships and Marketing informs the Editor-in-Chief and, for campaigns, the client.
3. A crisis is any situation that could seriously harm Mboko's reputation, a contributor's safety or a client relationship, such as a viral complaint or a hacked account. The Social Media and Audience Manager pauses all scheduled posts and informs the Head of Partnerships and Marketing and the Managing Director immediately.
4. A hacked account is also reported to the Finance and Operations Manager, who resets access.
