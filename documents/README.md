> **Fictional document.** Mboko Media Group is a fictional company created for a software engineering portfolio project. All people, clients, vendors, figures and records in this document are invented. Nothing here is legal, financial or employment advice.

## Purpose

This is the master index of Mboko Media Group's internal knowledge base. It lists every approved document, its ID, owning department, owner and purpose. The knowledge base is the data source for the Mboko Knowledge Assistant.

## How the knowledge base is organised

- Each of the six departments has its own folder. Company-wide references are in `company/`.
- Each document has a unique ID: `COM` for company-wide, `PROD` Production, `EDIT` Editorial, `CREA` Creative and Design, `MKT` Partnerships and Marketing, `HR` People and Administration, `FIN` Finance and Operations.
- The second part of the ID shows the document type: `REF` reference, `POL` policy, `SOP` procedure, `STD` standard, `IDX` index.
- Records in registers are current as at 15 October 2026.
- When two documents appear to conflict, the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001) applies to approvals, and the owning department's document applies to everything else. Document control rules are in the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001).
