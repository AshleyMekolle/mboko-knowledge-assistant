## Purpose

This policy explains how employees and freelancers request, check out, use and return Mboko Media Group's production equipment, and what to do when equipment is damaged or lost.

## Scope

This policy covers all cameras, lenses, audio kits, lighting, grips and storage media owned by Mboko and tagged with an `MBK-EQ` asset tag. Laptops and office IT equipment are covered by the asset inventory rules in the Procurement, Vendor and Invoice Procedure (FIN-SOP-001). Rented equipment from Akwa Rentals follows the same checkout and return steps as owned equipment.

## Requesting equipment

1. The borrower submits an equipment request in Mboko Board **at least 3 working days before the shoot day**, listing the asset tags, project code, shoot dates and location.
2. The Production Coordinator confirms availability within 1 working day.
3. For shoots outside Douala, the Head of Production must also approve the request.
4. If two requests clash, the Head of Production decides, giving priority to client deliverables and scheduled shoot days in the project register (PROD-POL-001).

Requests made with less than 3 working days' notice are accepted only if the equipment is free and the Production Coordinator agrees.

## Checking out equipment

1. Equipment is collected from the equipment room at the Douala office during working hours, 08:30 to 17:30.
2. The Production Coordinator and the borrower check each item together: it powers on, all parts are present and any existing damage is noted.
3. Both sign the checkout record in Mboko Board, which lists each asset tag, its condition and the agreed return date.
4. Memory cards are issued formatted and empty.

## Using equipment

- Equipment must never be left unattended in a vehicle or public place.
- Equipment kept overnight outside the office must be stored indoors in a locked room.
- Equipment must not be lent to anyone who is not on the checkout record.
- Equipment may only be used for Mboko productions.

## Returning equipment

1. Equipment must be returned **by 12:00 on the first working day after the last shoot day** on the checkout record.
2. Memory cards must not be formatted or erased until the footage has two verified copies, as required by the Footage Storage and Handover Procedure (PROD-SOP-001). The borrower returns cards to the Production Coordinator with the footage log.
3. The Production Coordinator checks each item and marks it as returned in Mboko Board. Any new damage is recorded.
4. Batteries are returned charged where possible.

Late returns are reported to the Head of Production. Repeated late returns may lead to the borrower losing booking rights.

## Damage, loss and theft

1. The borrower reports any damage, fault, loss or theft to the Production Coordinator **within 24 hours**.
2. The borrower also submits an incident report in Mboko Board, as described in the Production Planning, Scheduling and Project Register (PROD-POL-001).
3. Theft must also be reported to the local police, and the police report number is added to the incident report.
4. Damaged equipment is tagged "Do not use" and taken out of the pool until the Head of Production approves its repair or replacement.
5. Repair and replacement costs are approved under the limits in the Approval Authority Matrix (COM-POL-001).

## Exceptions and escalation

Exceptions to this policy, for example longer checkouts for multi-week shoot blocks such as Racines in Foumban, must be approved in advance by the Head of Production and recorded in Mboko Board.
