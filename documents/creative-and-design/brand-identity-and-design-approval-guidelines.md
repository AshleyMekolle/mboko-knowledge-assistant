## Purpose

These guidelines define Mboko Media Group's visual identity and the process for requesting, reviewing and approving creative work. They make sure everything Mboko publishes looks consistent and has been approved.

## Ownership

The Creative and Design department owns the brand identity guidelines. The Creative Director (Kevin Tabi) is the only person who may approve changes to the Mboko brand. All departments must follow these guidelines when they create documents, presentations, social posts or video graphics.

## Brand identity

### Logo

- The Mboko logo is the word "MBOKO" in bold capitals with a short ochre underline.
- Versions: full colour on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds or photography, and black for single-colour printing.
- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "M" on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 120 pixels wide on screens and 25 mm wide in print.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects to or place the logo on busy backgrounds.
- Master logo files are stored in Mboko Drive under `/Creative/Brand/Logo/`.

### Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Mboko Ochre | #C8781E | Primary brand colour, underline, highlights and buttons |
| Wouri Blue | #1F4E79 | Secondary colour, backgrounds and titles |
| Ink Black | #1A1A1A | Body text |
| Sand | #F2E6D0 | Light backgrounds |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text on dark backgrounds |

Each production may use one additional accent colour, approved by the Creative Director.

### Typography

| Use | Typeface |
|---|---|
| Headings, titles and thumbnails | Montserrat Bold |
| Body text, subtitles and captions | Source Sans 3 Regular |

Subtitles use Source Sans 3 in white with a 60% black background box, at a size readable on a mobile phone.

### Photography and video style

Natural light, warm tones and real people in real places. Contributors are shown with dignity. Staged scenes must not be presented as real events.

### Production identities

Each flagship production has its own title card and thumbnail template, stored in `/Creative/Brand/Productions/`: Mboa Makers, Rues de Douala, Market Voices and Racines. Thumbnails must use the template for their production.

## Using a client's or partner's logo

A client's or partner's logo may be used only when **all** of these conditions are met:
1. The client has given **written permission** for the specific use, such as a sponsor credit, a co-branded post or a campaign film. The Head of Partnerships and Marketing obtains this permission and stores it with the client contract.
2. The logo file comes directly from the client and is used according to the client's own brand guidelines.
3. The use matches what the contract allows. For example, Banque Sawa's sponsorship (CMP-2026-01) allows a sponsor credit on Mboa Makers Season 2 episodes and social posts, but not inside the editorial story.
4. The asset is approved by both the Creative Director and the Head of Partnerships and Marketing.
5. The asset is sent to the client for written approval before publication.

The Mboko logo and a client logo must never be combined into a new joint logo.

## Requesting creative work

1. The requester submits a creative brief in Mboko Board.
2. Deadlines for briefs:
   - **Standard assets** (thumbnails, single social graphics, title cards): at least **5 working days** before the asset is needed.
   - **Campaign packages** (multiple assets, motion graphics, client-facing material): at least **10 working days** before the asset is needed.
3. Every brief must include: project code, the asset and formats needed, the audience, the key message, languages (English, French or both), the deadline, required approvers and any client brand guidelines.
4. The Creative Director accepts the brief or asks for missing information within 1 working day.

## Design review and approval

1. **Self-check.** The designer checks the asset against these guidelines, the brief, spelling in both languages, and the licence status of any third-party material.
2. **Internal review.** The Creative Director reviews the asset within 2 working days and approves it or requests changes.
3. **Revision rounds.** Each brief includes two rounds of revisions. Additional rounds need the Creative Director's agreement and may delay the deadline.
4. **Editorial check.** Text on assets with factual claims is checked by Editorial under the Editorial Standards and Fact-Checking Policy (EDIT-POL-001).
5. **Client-facing assets.** The Head of Partnerships and Marketing also approves the asset and then sends it to the client for written approval.
6. **Approval record.** The Creative Director marks the asset "Approved" in Mboko Board and the final file is saved with the `FINAL` suffix described in the Asset Naming, Storage and Licensing Standard (CREA-STD-002).

Unapproved assets must not be published or sent to clients as final.
