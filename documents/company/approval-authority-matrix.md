> **Fictional document.** Mboko Media Group is a fictional company created for a software engineering portfolio project. All people, clients, vendors, figures and records in this document are invented. Nothing here is legal, financial or employment advice.

## Purpose

This matrix is the single source of truth for who may approve what at Mboko Media Group. Every other policy uses these limits. If another document appears to conflict with this matrix, this matrix applies and the conflict must be reported to the People and Administration Manager under the document control rules in the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001).

## General rules

- **No self-approval.** No employee may approve their own expense, purchase, leave or content. The next approver in the chain approves instead.
- **Department Head claims and purchases.** When a Department Head is the requester, the Managing Director approves in place of the Department Head.
- **Managing Director claims and purchases.** The Finance and Operations Manager reviews them, and they are reported to the Board of Directors each quarter.
- **Approvals are cumulative.** An expense of XAF 800,000 needs the Department Head, the Finance and Operations Manager and the Managing Director.
- **Evidence.** Every approval must be recorded in the system named in the matrix (Mboko Board, Mboko Ledger or MbokoHR). Verbal approval is not valid.
- **Amounts** include all taxes and are stated in XAF. All amounts are fictional.

## Financial approvals: expenses and purchases

These limits apply to a single expense claim, purchase or purchase order. Splitting a purchase into smaller amounts to stay under a limit is prohibited.

| Amount (XAF, including tax) | Required approvers | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 100,000 | Department Head | Mboko Ledger |
| 100,001 to 500,000 | Department Head and Finance and Operations Manager | Mboko Ledger |
| 500,001 to 2,000,000 | Department Head, Finance and Operations Manager and Managing Director | Mboko Ledger |
| Above 2,000,000 | Department Head, Finance and Operations Manager and Managing Director, and the cost must already be in an approved budget | Mboko Ledger |

**Example:** An expense of XAF 350,000 is approved by the requester's Department Head and the Finance and Operations Manager.

## Purchasing requirements

| Purchase value (XAF) | Purchase order | Quotations required |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 100,000 | Not required | Not required |
| 100,001 to 250,000 | Required | Not required |
| 250,001 to 1,000,000 | Required | 2 written quotations |
| Above 1,000,000 | Required | 3 written quotations |

Full purchasing rules are in the Procurement, Vendor and Invoice Procedure (FIN-SOP-001).

## Payments

- The Finance and Operations Officer prepares payments. The Finance and Operations Manager authorises them in the bank. The same person may never both prepare and authorise a payment.
- Payments above XAF 2,000,000 also need the Managing Director as second bank authoriser.

## Budget approvals

| Decision | Approver |
|---|---|
| Annual company budget | Managing Director, by 15 December each year |
| Project budget for a new production or branded project | Managing Director, after review by the Finance and Operations Manager |
| Project budget increase of more than 10% or more than XAF 500,000 (whichever is lower) | Managing Director |
| Moving money between budget lines within one project, up to XAF 500,000 | Finance and Operations Manager |

## Content and publication approvals

| Decision | Approver | Recorded in |
|---|---|---|
| Story idea greenlit for development | Editor-in-Chief and Head of Production | Mboko Board |
| Production Plan and shoot schedule | Head of Production | Mboko Board |
| Fact-Check Report complete | Researcher and Fact-Checker | Mboko Board |
| Final Editorial Sign-off on any published content | Editor-in-Chief | Mboko Board |
| High-risk content | Editor-in-Chief and Managing Director | Mboko Board |
| French or English translation and subtitles | Bilingual Editor | Mboko Board |
| Creative assets (thumbnails, graphics, titles) | Creative Director | Mboko Board |
| Client-facing creative assets | Creative Director and Head of Partnerships and Marketing | Mboko Board |
| Final publication of a branded or sponsored campaign | Head of Partnerships and Marketing, after the client's written approval and Final Editorial Sign-off | Mboko Board |
| Corrections to published content | Editor-in-Chief | Corrections Log |
| Retraction of published content | Editor-in-Chief and Managing Director | Corrections Log |

## Commercial approvals

| Decision | Approver |
|---|---|
| Sponsorship or campaign proposal sent to a client | Head of Partnerships and Marketing |
| Proposal with a total value above XAF 10,000,000 | Head of Partnerships and Marketing and Managing Director |
| Signing any client, sponsorship or partnership contract | Managing Director |
| Client change request that adds cost | Head of Partnerships and Marketing, with cost approved at the financial limits above |
