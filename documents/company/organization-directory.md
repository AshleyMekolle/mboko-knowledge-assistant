## Purpose

This directory lists every Mboko Media Group employee, their role, department and reporting line, and the responsibilities each department owns. Use it to find out who is responsible for a task or who to contact.

All people in this directory are fictional. The directory contains work roles only. It does not contain phone numbers, personal email addresses, home addresses or any other personal data.

## Role responsibilities

### Nadège Ekwalla, Managing Director
- Leads the company and sets annual priorities.
- Approves the annual budget, expenses and purchases above XAF 500,000, and high-risk content.
- Signs all client, sponsorship and partnership contracts.
- Decides on project delays of more than 5 working days to a client deliverable.

### Samuel Tchoumi, Head of Production
- Owns production planning, shoot scheduling, production safety and footage storage procedures.
- Approves Production Plans, shoot schedules and equipment use outside Douala.
- Producer of Mboa Makers Season 2 (MBK-MM-S02) and Racines Season 1 (MBK-RAC-S01).

### Brenda Ngum, Production Coordinator
- Coordinates shoots, crew, locations and call sheets.
- Is the equipment custodian: issues, checks and receives all production equipment.
- Producer of Rues de Douala Season 1 (MBK-RDD-S01) and the Kribi Coffee Collective film (MBK-BR-KCC-01).
- Prepares the Handover Package from Production to Editorial.

### Arnaud Fotso, Editor-in-Chief
- Owns editorial standards, fact-checking, consent and corrections policies.
- Gives Final Editorial Sign-off on all published content.
- Editorial lead for Market Voices Season 3 (MBK-MV-S03).

### Linda Achu, Researcher and Fact-Checker
- Researches stories and contributors.
- Fact-checks every script and article and produces the Fact-Check Report.
- Keeps the Corrections Log.

### Claire Mvondo, Bilingual Editor
- Translates and edits English and French content and produces subtitles.
- Signs off translations and arranges the second-language review.

### Kevin Tabi, Creative Director
- Owns the brand identity guidelines and the asset naming, storage and licensing standard.
- Approves all creative assets before publication.

### Grace Mbah, Senior Designer (Graphics and Motion)
- Designs thumbnails, social creatives, titles and motion graphics.
- Records licences for third-party music, images and footage in the creative asset register.

### Joëlle Essomba, Head of Partnerships and Marketing
- Manages clients, sponsors and partners and owns the sponsorship and campaign workflow.
- Gives final campaign publication approval for branded and sponsored campaigns.

### Patrick Njoya, Social Media and Audience Manager
- Schedules and publishes content on Mboko's social and video channels.
- Moderates comments and produces campaign performance reports.

### Ruth Fon, People and Administration Manager
- Manages recruitment, onboarding, offboarding, leave records, training and employee records.
- Owns the employee handbook and the document control rules for this knowledge base.

### Emmanuel Kamga, Finance and Operations Manager
- Owns all finance policies, the annual budget process and the monthly financial close.
- Approves expenses and purchases from XAF 100,001 and authorises payments.
- Owns company systems and IT accounts.

### Aïcha Bello, Finance and Operations Officer
- Processes expense claims, purchase orders and vendor invoices in Mboko Ledger.
- Is the petty cash custodian and maintains the asset inventory.
- Issues laptops and creates system accounts for new employees.

## Department ownership and boundaries

| Activity | Owning department | Other departments involved |
|---|---|---|
| Fact-checking documentaries, series and articles | Editorial | Production supplies transcripts and footage logs |
| Interview consent records | Editorial | Production collects signatures on shoot days |
| Shoot schedules and equipment | Production | Finance and Operations maintains the asset inventory |
| Footage storage | Production | Finance and Operations maintains MBK-NAS-01 and Mboko Archive |
| Brand identity guidelines | Creative and Design | All departments must follow them |
| Social media publishing | Partnerships and Marketing | Editorial and Creative and Design approve content first |
| Client approvals on branded content | Partnerships and Marketing | Production and Creative and Design make the changes |
| Employee onboarding | People and Administration | Finance and Operations sets up laptops and accounts |
| Purchases and vendor invoices | Finance and Operations | The requesting department raises the request |

A department that owns an activity also owns the governing document for it. When a task crosses departments, the owning department is responsible for the outcome and the other departments are responsible for completing their stated steps.
