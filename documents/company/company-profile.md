## Purpose

This document is the authoritative overview of Mboko Media Group. It describes what the company does, how it is organised, its flagship productions and the internal systems employees use. It also defines the terms and abbreviations used across the knowledge base.

## Company overview

Mboko Media Group is a fictional Cameroonian media and storytelling company headquartered in Douala, Cameroon. Mboko creates and distributes documentaries, interviews, digital video series, editorial features and branded campaigns about African people, culture, business, creativity and everyday life.

- **Tagline:** Our stories. Our world.
- **Headquarters:** Douala, Cameroon
- **Working languages:** English and French. Documents in this knowledge base are written in English. Content published to audiences may be in English, French or both, as defined in the Editorial Review, Bilingual Publishing and Corrections Procedure (EDIT-SOP-001).
- **Team size:** 13 employees, listed in the Organization Directory (COM-REF-002).

## Mission

Mboko Media Group's mission is to tell authentic, accurate and meaningful African stories that reflect the people and communities behind them.

## Core values

| Value | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Authenticity | Stories are told with the people who live them, not about them from a distance. |
| Accuracy | Every factual claim is checked before publication, and errors are corrected openly. |
| Respect | The dignity, consent and safety of contributors come before any deadline. |
| Community | Audiences are treated as a community that Mboko serves and listens to. |
| Craft | Sound, image, writing and design are held to a professional standard. |

## Glossary and acronyms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Approved for Publication | A status in Mboko Board meaning every required approval in EDIT-SOP-001 and, for campaigns, MKT-SOP-001 has been recorded. Content without this status must not be published or scheduled. |
| Call sheet | The document sent to cast and crew at least 48 hours before a shoot day with times, location, contacts and safety notes. |
| Consent record | The signed or recorded permission from a contributor, defined in the Interview Consent and Contributor Release Policy (EDIT-POL-002). |
| Department Head | The head of one of the six departments listed above. |
| EIC | Editor-in-Chief, the head of the Editorial department. |
| Fact-Check Report | The record produced by the Researcher and Fact-Checker that lists every factual claim in a script or article and how it was verified. |
| Final Editorial Sign-off | The Editor-in-Chief's recorded approval that content meets Mboko's editorial standards. |
| GRN | Goods Received Note, the record confirming that goods or services were received as ordered. |
| Handover Package | The set of files Production gives to Editorial after shooting, defined in PROD-SOP-001. |
| High-risk content | Content involving allegations against named people or organisations, minors, health or legal claims, or contributors who could be put at risk by publication. |
| MD | Managing Director. |
| NIU | Numéro d'Identifiant Unique, the Cameroonian taxpayer identification number required for vendors. |
| PO | Purchase order, raised in Mboko Ledger before a purchase is made. |
| Production Plan | The planning document required for every episode or shoot, defined in PROD-POL-001. |
| Working day | Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in Cameroon. |
| XAF | Central African CFA franc, the currency used for all amounts in this knowledge base. |

### Production workflow stages

Every project and episode in Mboko Board has exactly one of these stages: Idea, Research, Greenlit, Pre-production, Production, Post-production, Editorial Review, Approved for Publication, Scheduled, Published, Closed.
